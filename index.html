FamDose Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

FamDose is an app that helps families keep track of medicines and doses. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. We wrote it in simple words on purpose.

Who we are: FamDose Nagpur,Maharashtra,India.
Contact: famdose33@gmail.com

Important: FamDose is not a doctor and does not give medical advice. The AI Helper gives general information only. In an emergency, call 112 (national) or 108 (ambulance).

1. Information we collect

WhatWhy we need it
Your email address, name and password (the password is stored securely by our login service and we cannot read it)To create your account and sign you in.
Family members you add: name, relationship, birth year, preferred language, time zoneTo keep a separate medicine plan for each person.
Medicines and schedules: medicine name, strength, active ingredient, how and when to take it, number of tablets, start date, course lengthTo show your plan and remind you.
Dose history: whether a dose was taken, skipped or snoozed, and whenTo show history and to know when to alert a relative.
Health card (optional): blood group, allergies, long-term conditions, doctor name and phone, emergency noteSo your family can find it quickly in an emergency. You decide what to write.
Relatives for alerts (optional): their name, relationship and email addressTo send an email if a dose is not marked. We ask you to confirm that the relative agrees.
AI Helper: your questions and the answers, and the names of the medicines of the selected personTo answer your question and to show your chat history.
Photos of a medicine strip or prescription, only when you tap "Scan"To read the printed text and fill in the medicine form.
Daily count of AI questionsTo apply a fair daily limit.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or advertising ID. We do not show ads and we do not sell your data.

2. How reminders work

Medicine reminders are set on your phone and use your phone's notification settings. The app asks permission to send notifications. If you turn them off in your phone, reminders will not ring.

3. Who we share information with

We use trusted service providers to run FamDose. They process data only to provide their service:

  • Supabase stores our database and handles sign-in. Your data is stored securely and each account can only read its own data.
  • Google (Gemini API) answers AI Helper questions and reads scanned photos. When you use these features, your question, the medicine names of the selected person (for the AI Helper), or the photo (for Scan) is sent to Google. Please do not type information into the AI Helper that you do not want to share. Google handles this data under its own Gemini API terms and privacy policy.
  • Brevo sends emails (alerts to your relatives and password reset codes). The alert email contains only the person's name and the dose time. It does not contain the medicine name.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

4. Scanned photos

A photo is sent for reading and is not saved by us in a gallery or in storage. Always check the result before saving a medicine, because reading can make mistakes.

5. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete it any time (see section 6). Daily AI usage counts and email-code request records are small technical records and are removed when you delete your account or by regular clean-up.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Settings, then Delete my account. This permanently deletes your login and everything you added: family members, medicines, dose history, health cards, relatives for alerts and AI chats.
  • Delete a single AI chat: in the AI Helper menu, delete a chat from Recent chats.
  • Remove a relative from alerts: Profile, then Family Alerts, then remove.
  • Correct or see your data: you can edit your information in the app. You can also write to us at the email above to ask for a copy or a correction.

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 of India, you have the right to access, correct and erase your personal data and to complain about how it is handled. You can write to our grievance contact: [YOUR NAME], [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL]. We will reply within a reasonable time.

7. Security

Data is sent over secure connections and stored with access rules so that each person can only reach their own information. No system is 100% safe, so please use a strong password and keep your phone locked.

8. Children

FamDose accounts are for adults (18 years or older). An adult may keep a plan for a child in their family. We do not knowingly let children under 18 create an account.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
